How I Create Neon Reflections in My Environment Art

A lot of people ask how I achieve those wet-pavement neon reflection effects. Here's a quick breakdown of my workflow:


1. Base geometry — I model a flat plane with a subtle displacement for unevenness. The reflectivity shader is key here — I use a Glossiness value around 0.65-0.75 for that slightly blurred wet look.


2. Light placement — I place multiple area lights matching every neon sign color. Keeping them dim and letting the V-Ray GI do the heavy lifting gives the most realistic spread.


3. Post-processing — In Photoshop, I add a bloom layer (Screen blending, ~35% opacity) on top of all light sources, and a color grade with lifted blacks for that cinematic feel.


Hope this helps! Feel free to ask questions in the comments 👇
